# Quasaria landing page outline (approved copy)

1. Hero
Headline: Trade on the Stellar Decentralized Exchange.
Pitch: Quasaria is a client for the Stellar network. Send, receive, and trade assets on the Stellar network easily with Quasaria.
Visual: the animated quasar scene with the illustrative logo, a live XLM price ticker, and buttons to the Exchange and to Swap, plus "Create an account". Add a small "Testnet beta" badge until the live launch. Directly beneath the hero, a prominent "Your keys, your XLM." strip (it also stays in the account flow).

2. Markets
Headline: Every Stellar market in one view.
Pitch: Browse the top assets on the Stellar network with live prices, 24-hour moves, and volume, then trade any of them in one click.
Visual: an interactive table with asset logos, price, 24-hour change, and small price charts, plus an "as of" time on each row. Clicking a row opens it on the Trade page.

3. Trade
Headline: Tap into the whole network's liquidity.
Pitch: Quasaria trades directly on Stellar's built-in order book and liquidity pools, so you get the same deep liquidity every Stellar app shares from day one.
Visual: a live order book and swap widget preview, with a short animation of an order settling on the ledger in about five seconds.

4. Your keys, your coins
Headline: Your keys never leave your browser.
Pitch: When you create an account, your browser generates your secret key and public address, and we never see or store either. Nobody can freeze, lose, or hand over your funds but you.
Visual: a three-step animation (generate, back up, trade) and a callout that says "What we store about you: nothing."

5. Stablecoins
Headline: Every verified stablecoin, paired with XLM.
Pitch: Trade and provide liquidity for USDC, EURC, PYUSD, USDT0, AUDD, GYEN, and local-currency coins from Argentina to South Africa, each in its own XLM pool. We only list coins whose issuers confirm them, so impostor tokens stay out.
Visual: a logo grid with "Verified by issuer" badges, and a world map that highlights the local-currency coins.

6. Liquidity rewards
Headline: Earn fees by powering the pools.
Pitch: Add both sides of a pair to a Stellar-native liquidity pool and earn a share of every swap that pool makes. You choose the price bounds, and you can withdraw at any time.
Visual: a multi-asset pool browser (pairs, reserves, 24-hour volume, fee APR estimate) with deposit and withdraw screens, clearly labeled as an estimate and not a promise.

7. Holder rewards (GLACIERQ)
Headline: Hold GLACIERQ, get paid daily.
Pitch: Hold at least 1,000,000 GLACIERQ and receive GLACIERQ every day: 1 percent a month (12 percent a year) from 1 million tokens, the same rate from 10 million, and nothing under 1 million. Paid by plain payments from a finite reward reserve, with no smart contract. Rewards are not guaranteed, they put more tokens into circulation, and a daily cap can reduce them.
Visual: a tier table, a daily-reward estimator labeled as an estimate, and a plain finite-reserve, dilution and not-guaranteed disclosure.

8. Open data
Headline: Built on open, public data.
Pitch: Market prices and asset info come from the stellarchain.io public data feed, while every trade settles directly on Stellar's own network, so everything we show you can be checked.
Visual: a simple diagram of the data sources and settlement path, with stellarchain.io attribution.

9. Transparency and security
Headline: Nothing hidden, everything on-chain.
Pitch: Every Quasaria contract is public on the Stellar ledger, so anyone can inspect exactly how the pools and rewards work.
Visual: links to each contract on a block explorer, plus an honest audit status line that says "not yet audited" until an audit is done.

10. FAQ
Headline: Questions, answered.
Pitch: Short answers to what first-time visitors ask most, such as what happens if they lose their key, what fees are, and whether their money is safe.
Visual: an accordion list of questions.

11. Final call to action and footer
Headline: Your first trade is a few clicks away.
Pitch: Create an account in seconds and start trading on Stellar with full control of your funds.
Visual: repeat "Launch app" and "Create an account" buttons over the starfield. Footer carries the risk disclosure: Quasaria is experimental software, nothing on the site is financial advice, and liquidity pools and token rewards carry real risk of loss and may be regulated where you live.

12. Stellar news
Headline: Latest from Stellar.
Pitch: Protocol releases, developer updates, ecosystem news and live network status for Stellar, collected automatically from the Stellar Development Foundation, GitHub release notes, the Stellar status page and the community, every 3 hours.
Visual: a network status line, category chips and six news cards (headline, source, local time, a short excerpt, category tags and an optional thumbnail) that open the original article in a new tab, an "Updated" time, the source list, a note that news comes from third-party sources and isn't endorsement or financial advice, and a "See all news" link to the /news page.
